Stuff people ask

Questions

What if I don't like the options you send?
If we missed the mark — wrong style, wrong scale, wrong vibe — we'll redo it for free. If we nailed the brief and you just want more options, another round is $25 (Quick Fix) or $50 (Room Refresh).
What if the listing sells before I can grab it?
Marketplace moves fast. Message your top picks within 24 hours of getting your finds — that's the rule. If your favorite sells before you can grab it, we'll send one free replacement option. After that, we can find more for $25.
How fast do I actually need to act?
Fast. Within a day of getting your finds is ideal. The good stuff sells in hours, not days. We'll send you a copy-paste seller message so you don't have to think — just send it.
Where do you source from?
Wherever the good stuff is, which is usually secondhand — Facebook Marketplace, AptDeco, Kaiyo, Chairish — plus Etsy and small makers for the handmade end. If the right answer for your space happens to be something new, we'll say so. We're not precious about it. We're just not sending you a $900 particle-board sofa.
Do I have to do anything besides fill out the form?
Not really. We send you the finds, you message the sellers (we give you the template), you arrange pickup. We don't transact for you — you handle the actual buying.
How do I pay?
Fill out the form first. We'll look it over and send you a Venmo request, usually within a few hours — or pay @betterthanabox directly if you'd rather not wait. Work starts once it's paid.
Can I get a refund?
No refunds once we've delivered your finds, but we're not going to leave you hanging. If something's off, tell us — we'll fix it. The free revision is there for exactly this.
Why should I pay for this when I could just ask my aesthetic friend?
Because you haven't yet, and you're not going to. Also, your sister has stopped opening your texts that start with "ok which rug."
